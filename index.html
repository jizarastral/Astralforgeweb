FAQ

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Is this just a chatbot?

No. AstralForge builds AI workflows around your actual business processes, customer questions and lead qualification requirements.

Does it replace my employees?

Not necessarily. It handles repetitive conversations so your staff can focus on higher-value work.

Can it capture leads?

Yes, when configured for lead capture (name, phone, email, requirements, urgency).

Can it hand conversations to a human?

Yes, where the workflow supports human escalation with context.

Can you build it for my industry?

Yes, subject to complexity and the information you provide.

Can it work with my existing website?

Implementation depends on your website technology and required integration. We confirm this during the audit.

Can it use my business information?

Yes. The AI is configured around approved information: services, FAQs, policies and workflows.

Can I cancel?

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled before the next billing cycle once active; setup fees for work already delivered are non-refundable. Exact terms are confirmed in writing before payment.

Is AI always accurate?

No. No AI system is infallible. Important business rules must be configured carefully, and sensitive situations should escalate to humans.
