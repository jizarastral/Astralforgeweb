FAQ

Straight answers

What exactly is an AI employee?

An AI employee is an AI-powered system configured to handle specific business tasks such as answering customer questions, qualifying enquiries, capturing leads or collecting booking requests.

Does it replace my employees?

No. The goal is to automate repetitive work and help your team handle customer enquiries more efficiently. Human staff can remain responsible for situations requiring judgment or escalation.

Can it learn my business?

It can be configured using information you provide, such as services, FAQs, policies, pricing and business information. The exact knowledge setup depends on the deployment.

Can it capture leads?

Yes. Lead capture can be designed into the conversation flow.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

WhatsApp availability depends on the specific implementation and approved integrations. We will confirm the required setup before deployment.

Can it book appointments?

Booking workflows can be implemented where the required booking/calendar integration is available.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. AstralForge handles the technical setup.

How much does it cost?

Initial packages start from AED 999 setup plus AED 299/month. Custom requirements may have different pricing.

Is AI always accurate?

No AI system is perfect. AstralForge deployments should include controlled business knowledge, testing, escalation paths and human oversight where appropriate.

Where is my data stored?

Data handling depends on the deployment architecture and services used. We will clearly document the relevant data handling arrangements for your implementation.
