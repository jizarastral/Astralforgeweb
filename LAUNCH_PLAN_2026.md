# ASTRAL FORGE — THE FORGE OPENS
## AI-first website redesign & launch working plan
**Created:** 2026-11-09 (Asia/Dubai)
**Status:** Internal launch concept; NOT a product release or paid media authorization.
**Canonical target:** https://astralforgeae.com/
**GitHub source:** jizarastral/Astralforgeweb

### Positioning
**DESIGNING THE FUTURE.**
Human-first AI software. Tools that make builders more capable while keeping humans in control.
Headline promise: **Build with intelligence. Stay in control.**
Do NOT claim "world's first," "unhackable," "best secured ever," shipped availability, AI breakthroughs or dates without evidence.

### Website architecture
- AI-first animated hero (orbital visual, starfield, restrained UI motion).
- Five focus products: Astral Terminal, CAD Viewer, Astral Clipper, Astral ERP, AI Generator.
- Each product displays an accurate development/testing status; no premature downloads or fabricated testimonials.
- Security and user agency are leading trust signals, framed as design commitments, not completed certifications.
- Clear CTA to request early access via existing company email. This is not a backend waitlist and does not automatically capture leads.
- Mobile-friendly, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, static site; no third-party tracking bundled into the prototype.
- Old home/service/shop-oriented top-level routes redirect to the new product/vision/launch anchors in the preview branch.
- Existing Astral chat route is preserved pending explicit migration review.

### The launch (first public product, then portfolio)
1. **Signal (prelaunch):** Founder's concept video, an animated site teaser and 3 product-specific stories. Start collecting qualified early-access requests with clear data-use disclosure.
2. **Proof (private beta):** One release candidate is validated on real environments. Invite a small opted-in test group. Publish accurate demo clips, supported platforms and known limitations.
3. **The Forge Opens (launch event):** A tightly produced 8–12 minute launch film + real, unscripted demos. Show a builder moving from prompt to action with explicit approvals and reproducible workflows. Never fake live results.
4. **Activation (first 30 days):** Publish changelogs, security information, onboarding, support and documentation. Measure qualified activations, retention, crash-free sessions and user trust signals rather than impressions alone.

### JustAd — possible partnership / pilot
Reference: https://just.ad/ ; multi-platform ads management and reporting.
- Propose a **founding launch pilot**, not an assumed partnership.
- Request: platform demo, written quote, all fees, access/ownership of ad accounts, approval controls, audience/geo targeting, reporting/export rights, data processing/privacy terms, campaign budget caps, refund and cancellation terms.
- Their pricing page may differ from the signup checkout quote; verify before payment.
- **Pilot suggestion** (only once launch landing + real demo + consented attribution is ready): AED 1,500–3,000 media testing budget over two weeks, excluding service/platform fees.
- Week 1: two audiences (developers/technical founders, operations/SMB decision makers) × 2–3 grounded creatives, one clear CTA each.
- Week 2: pause creatives with poor qualified conversion; move budget only to proven conversion paths.
- Do not assume paid traffic is profitable; ask for transparent account-level metrics and controls.
- Minimum measurement: landing visits, product-page clicks, qualified early-access requests, consented follow-up, beta activations and 7-day usage.
- Avoid financed advertising until unit economics, repayment terms and downside are independently assessed.

### Go / no-go launch gate
- Required MVP functional tests PASS on supported platforms; limitation disclosures written.
- Threat model, least privilege, keyring secrets, command approvals, CSP, no silent external links.
- Third-party dependency/security scanning, signed package/update mechanism, pen test and remediation of critical/high findings.
- Installer, onboarding, rollback, support and privacy notices tested.
- Website domain/DNS/TLS, analytics consent, uptime, mobile/a11y and contact workflow verified.
- Separate authorized production deployment and campaign spend approvals.
- Do not imply a product is available until its download and support infrastructure exist.
