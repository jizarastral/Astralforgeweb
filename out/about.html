THE NAME BEHIND ASTRAL

Jizar Rawther Podikunju

“Astral” is the creative identity. Astral Forge is the work taking shape behind it.

The path hasn't been linear. Games and digital worlds were part of the exploration. Over time, the focus shifted toward understanding how systems work — and building tools rather than only using them.

That shift came with constraints: limited resources, imperfect iterations, failed approaches, and lessons from real work. It also produced something worth protecting: the discipline to verify an idea before calling it a product.

Astral Forge is still being built. We would rather show you a genuinely useful release than publish an exaggerated promise.
